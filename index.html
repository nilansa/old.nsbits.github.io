R.012025—NOW

MS research · ongoing

HashSamplers

Non-uniform data sampling with hash-signatures for faster large-model training

I am investigating CPU-side data selection that retrieves useful training chunks from inexpensive signatures built from LM-head gradient proxies, hidden states, and loss statistics. The goal is better learning under a fixed token and compute budget.

  • Data selection
  • LLM pretraining
  • LSH / ANN
  • ML systems

R.022026

Research manuscript

Low-Rank Roads to Misalignment

Tracing emergent-misalignment directions across LoRA and full fine-tuning

With Dheeraj Hegde, I study whether behavioural directions are geometrically conserved across training regimes using residual-stream vectors, activation steering, projection ablation, and same-norm random controls.

  • LLM safety
  • Activation steering
  • LoRA
  • Representation geometry

R.032026

Independent study · completed

Devanagari as a latent bridge

Probing internal scripts in multilingual Indic language models

With Margamitra Bhattacharya, I adapted RomanLens-style probing to test whether Indic models route translation through Roman, Devanagari, or Bengali-script representations across layers and tasks.

  • Mechanistic interpretability
  • Multilingual NLP
  • Logit lens
  • Indic LMs

About

Curious about the path from training signal to model behaviour.

I am an MS by Research student in Computer Science and Engineering at IIIT Hyderabad. My current work asks how we can choose better pretraining data, inspect what language models represent internally, and evaluate when those representations lead to surprising behaviour.

I previously worked on recommender systems at Amazon, privacy tooling at the University of Surrey, and production systems across financial services, agriculture, and critical software.
